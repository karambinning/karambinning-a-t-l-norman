What we do

Butchery that hasn't
cut a corner.

A traditional butcher's counter with high quality meat and good quality pastries. Cuts prepared in the shop. Pies on the counter through the morning. The kind of service that comes from a lifetime in the trade.

The shop has built its reputation on Princes Avenue over more than thirty years. Some of the families who shop here have done so for two generations.
