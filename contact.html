Getting here

Three ways onto the avenue.

On foot or by bike from the centre of Hull or from the Newland Avenue side — Princes Avenue runs roughly north–south between Chanterlands Avenue and Spring Bank West, with the shop towards the southern end.

By bus — several Stagecoach routes serve Spring Bank and Princes Avenue. Ask the driver for the Princes Avenue stretch and you'll be a short walk from the shop.

By car — Princes Avenue has on-street parking down its length. Check the bay signs for the day's restrictions.
