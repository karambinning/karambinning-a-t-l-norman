About the shop

A proper butcher's,
quietly kept.

For more than thirty years T L Norman has traded from 72 Princes Avenue. The shop is run today by Chris and Tracy — a lifetime in the trade and the next generation of a customer book that goes back further still.

The shop

On Princes Avenue,
since before it was
foodie.

Princes Avenue is one of Hull's best-known food streets — a long ribbon of independent cafés, bakers, delis and restaurants running off Chanterlands and Spring Bank. T L Norman has been part of that street for the better part of a generation: a proper butcher's shop on a street that's grown around it.

The food guides for the avenue still list us among the names worth knowing. We are happy to keep that company.

Run by

Chris & Tracy.
Behind the counter.

The shop is run day-to-day by Chris and Tracy. Between them there is a lifetime of butchery — apprenticeships, blocks, knives kept properly, customers learned by name. You'll find one or both of them in the shop most days.

The trade isn't a posture and it isn't a brand: it's a job done well, in front of you, with your order in mind. If you need something cut, ask. If you need it boned and tied, ask. If you don't know what you need for what you're cooking, ask — that's half the point of a butcher.

The shop is what it is because the people behind the counter spent their working lives learning to do it properly. — A note from the shop

What we believe in

The standards on the wall.

01

High quality meat.

The counter only carries meat we'd take home. It is the simplest rule in the shop and the one that decides everything else.

02

Good quality pastries.

The pies on the counter are part of the reason people come — pork, huntsman, sausage and tom, sausage and egg, bacon and egg, around four pounds fifty each.

03

Hygiene, properly kept.

Rated 5 by Hull City Council at the most recent food hygiene inspection in January 2026 — the top rating, for the third inspection area as well as the first.

04

A long memory.

Some of our customers have come here for over thirty years — themselves, then their children. That is the only review of a butcher's that really matters.
